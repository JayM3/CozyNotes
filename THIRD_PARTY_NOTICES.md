## Software dependencies

JavaScript dependencies are declared in `package.json` and resolved in `package-lock.json`. Rust dependencies are declared in `src-tauri/Cargo.toml` and resolved in `src-tauri/Cargo.lock`. Their license texts are supplied with the installed packages or crates. The font licenses above are not a complete inventory of software dependency licenses; review the dependencies and include their required notices when preparing a binary distribution.
